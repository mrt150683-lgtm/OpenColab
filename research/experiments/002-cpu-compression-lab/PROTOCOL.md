# OpenColab lab 002: Can we keep useful accuracy below 10% of dense bytes?

Private exploratory lab. Do not represent it as publicly preregistered. Development choices use the training-derived validation split on seeds 11, 23, 47. Freeze one global recipe per lane at each qualifying cap before opening confirmation outputs. Confirmation uses new training seeds 101, 103, 107. The canonical test examples are reused from Challenge 001, not new data, and are not used to choose recipes in this lab. Also test fixed one-pixel horizontal shift and Gaussian pixel noise on the confirmation test images. Neither shift is used for recipe selection.

Primary: each seed loses ≤0.5 percentage points versus dense at ≤10% actual serialized bytes. Secondary: same accuracy criterion at ≤12% bytes. Compare post-training methods separately from methods that use labels and refitting. Do not call difference in size a speed or VRAM gain. Report all seeds, per-class accuracy, dense-relative degradation by class, and post-hoc deviations. Large degradation on shifted images is a caveat even if the clean gate passes.

Recipe selection: within each lane and cap, maximize minimum development seed accuracy difference vs its dense checkpoint; tie-break by mean difference, then smaller maximum bytes, then deterministic recipe string. Only complete recipes measured across all dev seeds are eligible. No retuning after confirmation; report negative results and identify what to test next. For refitting compare at least one selected recipe against its identical no-refit configuration and the prior all-4-bit baseline.
